HOW TO READ THIS RESULT

The opponent panel comes from the same training run. These results describe this development panel, not general playing strength. The displayed model is a saved snapshot, not a live training feed. Confidence intervals resample whole engine-seed blocks; a single block cannot provide one.

Method and provenance

Win share splits ties equally. The reward is the final VP margin against the strongest rival. Failed games are retained and invalidate progress comparisons. The panel covers every ordered opponent pair and all three seats.
