Self-play evaluation — Seed 47

Training seed 47; 262,144 transitions. Evaluation seed 39001, 40 engine-seed blocks and 480 attempted games per model against checkpoints at 65,536 and 131,072 transitions. This experiment predates feature semantics v3: science, chain and production observations were subsequently corrected. The results show learning with limited strategic diversity; they do not establish the effect of those corrections or general playing strength. Data-quality limitation: initial: 479/480 games completed. See the data-quality view; incomplete results are not a fully paired baseline comparison.
